# Engineer ladder

What each level means for the people we interview. Levels run lowest first, and every anchor and finding in the pack names one of them by id.

## Junior {#junior}

Delivers well-scoped work with guidance. Explains what they did, and asks when they are stuck.

## Mid {#mid}

Delivers features on their own, edge cases included. States the assumption they are making, and names what their fix leaves behind.

## Senior {#senior}

Fixes the class of problem, not the instance. Compares options with a cost for each, and brings other engineers along.
