## Ask

When would you use a `<button>` and when an `<a>`? Walk me through a case where the choice is not obvious.

## Look for

### junior

- Says links go somewhere and buttons do something
- Avoids a `div` with a click handler

### mid

- Names the keyboard and screen reader differences
- Chooses for a link that opens a modal, and says why #judgment

### senior

- Explains how to keep a design system from mixing the two #judgment

## Follow-ups

- What breaks if a link opens a modal?
- How would you style a link to look like a button?
